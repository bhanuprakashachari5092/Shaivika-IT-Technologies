FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions from Global Clients

How do you manage international time zones?

Our remote-first schedule includes overlapping business hours with North America, the UK, Europe, the Middle East, and Australia. We rely on clear asynchronous sprint documentation, shared staging links, and scheduled check-in calls to ensure smooth coordination.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, git repositories, design assets, and database schemas. Everything is transferred to your private organization upon project completion.

What happens after the product is launched?

We offer post-launch warranty support to resolve any unexpected bugs, as well as ongoing monthly sprint retainers for roadmap additions, cloud performance tuning, and technical iterations.

Can you modernize an existing legacy application?

Yes. We regularly conduct code audits, refactor legacy backends, modernize outdated frontends into modern React/Next.js architectures, and implement automated CI/CD pipelines without interrupting current users.
