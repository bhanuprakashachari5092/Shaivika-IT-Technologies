CASE STUDY — HEALTHCARE SAAS & AI COMMERCE

Hima Bindu Opticals: Intelligent Optical Clinic ERP & Digital Storefront

How SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered a unified optical clinic practice management system and customer storefront, integrating Google Gemini AI for virtual consultations, prescription telemetry, and real-time inventory synchronization.

Product Type

Healthcare & Optical ERP

Core Domain

AI Clinical Advice & Eyewear Store

Key Technologies

React, TypeScript, Gemini AI, Firebase

Delivery Scope

Cloud Web Platform + Desktop ERP

01 — OVERVIEW

Modernizing Optical Practice Management with Intelligent Automation

Independent optical clinics and eyewear retailers typically balance two disjointed systems: retail frame sales and clinical patient consultations. Paper prescriptions, phone appointments, and manual inventory tracking routinely lead to lost revenue and customer wait times.

Hima Bindu Opticals partnered with SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES to develop a complete digital ecosystem. The solution provides a patient-facing web storefront with an embedded Google Gemini AI optical assistant, paired with an operational clinic dashboard for appointments, prescription records, and live stock synchronization.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

Catalog Fragmentation, Prescription Errors, and Patient Consultation Friction

Key operational pain points addressed during engineering:

  • •
    Online Eyewear Decision Paralysis: Customers often abandon optical sites because they cannot determine which frame geometry or lens coating suits their specific visual symptoms.
  • •
    Prescription Data Capture: Optical prescriptions require recording spherical, cylindrical, axis, and pupillary distance values with zero room for transcription errors.
  • •
    Two-Way Inventory Sync: In-store physical purchases needed to immediately synchronize with the web catalog to prevent double-booking out-of-stock designer frames.
03 — AI-FIRST CLINICAL CONSULTATION

Google Gemini AI Optical Advisor

We integrated Google Gemini AI directly into the client storefront. The AI assistant conducts preliminary lifestyle and symptom questionnaires (computer vision strain, blue-light exposure, outdoor UV needs, face shape), recommending specific frame models and lens technologies before directing the customer to book an in-person eye exam.

04 — DELIVERED CAPABILITIES

Core System Features

✓ Virtual AI Optical Consultation: Personalized lens and frame recommendations tailored to customer daily usage.
✓ Digital Prescription Telemetry: Secure database for patient history, lens measurements, and doctor notes.
✓ Direct Clinic Appointment Dispatch: Instant scheduling with SMS and email confirmations.
✓ Hybrid Desktop & Web Deployment: Built with Vite and packaged with Electron for counter POS and clinic desktop operation.
05 — RESULTS & SOURCE REPOSITORY

Operational Efficiency & Open Source Access

The platform eliminated manual prescription paperwork, elevated patient appointment booking conversion, and enabled seamless frame inventory management.
