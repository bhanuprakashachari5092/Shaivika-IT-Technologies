CASE STUDY — MULTI-VENDOR MARKETPLACE

Vendor99 (HomeQuik): Multi-Vendor Home Services & Marketplace Ecosystem

How SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered an end-to-end multi-vendor services marketplace connecting consumers with verified home service contractors, real estate listings, and localized professional trades.

Product Type

Two-Sided Marketplace

Core Domain

Vendor Onboarding & Booking

Key Technologies

React, Node.js, Express, REST APIs

Delivery Scope

Full-Stack Marketplace Architecture

01 — OVERVIEW

Connecting Homeowners with Trusted Local Service Vendors

Home services (maintenance, electrical, plumbing, painting, remodeling, and property services) remain highly fragmented. Homeowners struggle to compare verified providers, obtain transparent estimates, and track booking commitments, while independent vendors lack the digital infrastructure to capture, manage, and dispatch service leads.

Vendor99 was designed to bridge this divide through a high-performance two-sided marketplace. SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered both client-facing discovery storefronts and vendor operational dashboards with secure booking pipelines and real-time inquiry telemetry.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

High Latency, Lead Leakage, and Category Complexity

Building a scalable local services marketplace required solving several fundamental engineering problems:

  • •
    High-Friction Vendor Onboarding: Service providers needed an intuitive workflow to list credentials, service radii, pricing models, and portfolio galleries without technical overhead.
  • •
    Real-Time Lead Routing & Notification: Preventing lead decay by instantly routing incoming booking inquiries to qualified local contractors via SMS and WhatsApp triggers.
  • •
    Sub-Second Location-Based Discovery: Instant search filtering across hundreds of service categories and geographic pin-codes.
03 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Decoupled REST API & Modular Microservice Design

We implemented a decoupled architecture featuring a React SPA client for responsive consumer browsing, backed by an Express/Node.js API gateway that handles auth, vendor catalog schemas, booking events, and lead lifecycle management.

04 — DELIVERED CAPABILITIES

Marketplace Features & Infrastructure

✓ Categorized Service Directories: Deep hierarchy covering electrical, plumbing, carpentry, renovations, and property maintenance.
✓ Vendor Management Portal: Profile customization, service rate management, customer reviews, and lead acceptance status.
✓ Instant Booking & Inquiry Engine: Frictionless consumer checkout with direct connection to service providers.
✓ Admin Central Command: Platform-wide oversight of active bookings, dispute resolutions, and vendor verification approvals.
05 — RESULTS & SOURCE REPOSITORY

Production Deployment & Live Impact

Vendor99 successfully streamlined local service discovery into an intuitive digital experience with sub-second page loads and zero booking leakage.
