CASE STUDY — EDTECH & CAMPUS MANAGEMENT

Student Attendance & Campus Academic Management Platform

How SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered a high-reliability institutional attendance and academic tracking system providing role-based workflows for college administration, professors, and students.

Product Type

Academic ERP Platform

Core Domain

Attendance Telemetry & Records

Key Technologies

React, Node.js, Express, REST APIs

Delivery Scope

Full-Stack Campus Portal Architecture

01 — OVERVIEW

Eliminating Paper Logs with Instant Academic Telemetry

Educational institutions and universities struggle with manual attendance registries, delayed shortage notifications, and tedious semester-end auditing. Faculty members lose valuable lecture time calling out roll calls, while students often remain unaware of critical attendance shortages until examination eligibility is compromised.

SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered an end-to-end Student Attendance & Academic Management Platform to digitize attendance marking, automate shortage alerts, and provide real-time compliance reports for institutional leadership.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

Proxy Attendance, Batch Segmentation, and Timely Alerts

The institutional platform needed to address critical operational requirements:

  • •
    Friction-Free Class Attendance Marking: Enabling professors to record and submit attendance for 60+ students in under 30 seconds from mobile or laptop devices.
  • •
    Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict permission isolation separating Department Heads, Subject Faculty, and Students.
  • •
    Automated Threshold Telemetry: Real-time alerts when student attendance dips below the 75% university examination threshold.
03 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

High-Concurrency API Gateway & Audit Pipeline

We developed a lightweight, decoupled web platform with a React front-end communicating with a high-throughput Node.js backend. All attendance submissions generate immutable audit timestamps, preventing retroactive tampering and ensuring regulatory compliance.

04 — DELIVERED CAPABILITIES

Platform Features & Modules

✓ One-Tap Faculty Marking: Rapid multi-student toggle interface with absent/present batch defaults.
✓ Student Self-Service Portal: Personal attendance percentages per subject with projected class requirements.
✓ Medical Leave & Duty Leave Approvals: Digital leave submission and approval pipeline for faculty heads.
✓ Institutional Excel & PDF Exports: One-click generation of university-format attendance ledgers and hall ticket eligibility lists.
05 — RESULTS & SOURCE REPOSITORY

Institutional Efficiency & Open Codebase

The platform eliminated hundreds of hours of manual ledger compilation per semester, giving administrators and students transparent, real-time academic telemetry.
