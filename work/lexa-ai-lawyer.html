CASE STUDY — LEGALTECH & MULTI-AGENT AI

AI LeXa Lawyer: Autonomous Multi-Agent Legal Intelligence Platform

How SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered an autonomous legal consultation platform combining multi-agent LLM analysis, automated FIR document drafting, secure evidence vaulting, and automated legal notice distribution.

Product Type

Autonomous Legal SaaS

Core Domain

Multi-Agent Legal Intelligence

Key Technologies

React, TypeScript, Supabase, LLMs

Delivery Scope

Architecture, AI Agents & Cloud Workflows

01 — OVERVIEW

Democratizing Access to Justice with Intelligent AI Systems

Navigating the legal system is notoriously expensive, intimidating, and opaque for citizens and small businesses alike. Most individuals do not know which statutory provisions apply to their grievance, how to correctly format a formal First Information Report (FIR), or what specific evidence is required to substantiate a legal claim.

AI LeXa Lawyer was created to transform legal assistance into an accessible, conversational experience. SHAIVIKA IT TECHNOLOGIES engineered the complete full-stack platform — combining an intuitive conversational interface, an orchestration layer of specialized AI legal agents, cloud evidence storage, and automated document compilation into production-ready PDF legal briefs.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

High Legal Costs, Complex Jargon, and Unstructured Evidence

Developing an autonomous legal assistant presents unique technical and architectural hurdles:

  • •
    Eliminating Hallucinations in Statutory References: Legal citations cannot afford creative fabrication. Every legal clause and procedural step must be grounded strictly in authentic penal and civil statutes.
  • •
    Translating Conversational Input to Legal Documentation: Everyday users describe events emotionally and chronologically, while police authorities and courts mandate precise legal structure, jurisdiction identification, and explicit penal sections.
  • •
    Confidential Multi-Format Evidence Vault: Managing sensitive multimedia proof (audio records, screenshots, signed contracts, PDFs) securely with role-based access control.
03 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Multi-Agent Legal Reasoning Pipeline

Instead of relying on a monolithic prompt, we engineered a collaborative multi-agent architecture where distinct specialized agents process user claims sequentially:

Agent 1: Ingestion & Triage

Extracts chronological timelines, parties involved, financial loss, and incident location from plain English dialog.

Agent 2: Statutory Statute Matcher

Maps extracted violations against penal provisions, civil law, cybercrime frameworks, and consumer protection acts.

Agent 3: Legal Risk Assessor

Evaluates evidential gaps, jurisdiction requirements, limitation periods, and potential defense arguments.

Agent 4: FIR & Notice Generator

Compiles formal affidavits, police FIR complaints, or legal notice drafts ready for immediate filing.

04 — DELIVERED CAPABILITIES

Key Features & Technical Highlights

✓ Natural Language Legal Chat: Step-by-step interactive triage accessible in simple terminology without legal gatekeeping.
✓ Automated FIR Generator: Generates professionally structured, jurisdiction-compliant FIR drafts in under 3 minutes.
✓ Secure Evidence Vault: Encrypted storage for images, voice memos, witness statements, and contracts with audit hashing.
✓ Automated PDF & Email Dispatch: Serverless Edge triggers automatically compile formatted PDFs and dispatch them to the user's verified email.
✓ Admin & Telemetry Dashboard: Case triage monitoring, analytics on prevalent grievance categories, and real-time processing metrics.
